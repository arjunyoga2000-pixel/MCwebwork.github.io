FAQ

Common questions

How long does it take to build a website?

A standard business website takes 7–14 working days and an e-commerce website 15–25 days. We share a clear timeline before we start.

What is included in the free hosting?

The first year of domain registration, hosting and SSL certificate are included. After that, renewal typically costs ₹2,000–₹4,000 per year.

Do I need technical knowledge to manage my website?

No. We show you how to update text, images and products, and you get 1 month of free support.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can modernise your current site and keep your domain and content.

What payment methods do you accept?

UPI, bank transfer, Razorpay and cash, usually 50% advance and 50% on delivery.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every site is mobile-first and tested on Android and iOS.
